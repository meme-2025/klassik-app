Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sacrifice Event?

A sacrifice event is a way for early supporters to show commitment to the project. You send KAS (which gets permanently burned), and in return, you receive points that determine your token allocation at TGE. It's not an investment - it's a demonstration of support.

How are points calculated?

Points are calculated based on your sacrifice amount and tier. Bronze: 2,500 pts/1K KAS (+25%), Silver: 3,750 pts/1K KAS (+50%), Gold: 4,375 pts/1K KAS (+75%), Diamond: 5,000 pts/1K KAS (+100%). Early sacrifices may receive additional bonuses.

When will I receive my tokens?

Tokens will be distributed at the Token Generation Event (TGE), which will be announced after the sacrifice event ends. Your allocation will be proportional to your points vs total points in the system.

Can I sacrifice multiple times?

Yes! You can make multiple sacrifices. All your points will be accumulated. Making larger individual sacrifices may unlock higher tiers with better multipliers.

What happens to the sacrificed KAS?

All sacrificed KAS is permanently burned (sent to a provably unspendable address). This reduces the total KAS supply and demonstrates genuine support for the project. You can verify all burns on the Kaspa blockchain.

Is there a minimum or maximum?

Minimum sacrifice: 1,000 KAS (Bronze tier). There is no maximum - the more you sacrifice, the higher your tier and bonus multiplier. Diamond tier starts at 100,000 KAS with +100% bonus.
