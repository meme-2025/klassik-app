Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Klassik

What is Klassik?

Klassik is a comprehensive DeFi platform built on the Kaspa blockchain. We provide a decentralized exchange (DEX), staking platform, blockchain explorer, and developer tools - all leveraging Kaspa's revolutionary BlockDAG technology for ultra-fast transactions and low fees.

How does the Sacrifice event work?

The Sacrifice event allows early supporters to contribute KAS tokens in exchange for KHUB token allocation. Sacrificed KAS is permanently burned, creating scarcity. Early participants receive bonus allocations (25-100% depending on tier). All transactions are transparent and verifiable on-chain.

Is Klassik audited and safe?

Yes! All our smart contracts are audited by CertiK, a leading blockchain security firm. We use a non-custodial design, meaning you always control your funds. Our code is open source and can be verified by anyone on GitHub.

What are the fees on Klassik?

Thanks to Kaspa's efficient architecture, transaction fees are minimal - typically around $0.0001 per transaction. Trading fees on our DEX are competitive at 0.3%, with a portion distributed to liquidity providers.

How can I participate?

Simply connect your MetaMask wallet using the "Connect Wallet" button. If you don't have a Kaspa-compatible wallet yet, we provide a guide to set one up. Once connected, you can participate in the Sacrifice event, trade, stake, and access all platform features.

When is the token launch?

The Token Generation Event (TGE) is scheduled for Q1 2025, following the completion of the Sacrifice phase. Exact dates will be announced on our official Discord and Twitter channels. Join our community to stay updated!
